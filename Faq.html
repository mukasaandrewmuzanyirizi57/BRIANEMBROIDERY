Frequently Asked Questions

Simple answers before you order

Do I need to bring my own shirt, uniform or cap?

No. You can bring your own, or ask us to help you find one. Bringing your own is often cheaper.

What is the minimum order?

There is no minimum order. One cap or 500 uniforms, we take it.

How much does embroidery cost?

Shirt embroidery starts from 2,000 UGX. Uniform embroidery starts from 2,500 UGX. Cap embroidery starts from 7,000 UGX. Digitizing is 20,000 UGX, paid once.

How long does my order take?

It depends on how many pieces and how busy we are. We tell you the number of days before you pay.

Do you need my logo as a file?

A file is good, but not a must. Bring it on your phone, on paper, or just describe it. We will help you turn it into a design that can be stitched.

Where are you found?

Jemba Plaza, Second Floor, Room K132, Kiyembe Lane / Luwum Street, Kampala. We are open Monday to Saturday, 8:00am to 8:00pm.

Still have a question?

Call us or send a message. We reply the same day.
