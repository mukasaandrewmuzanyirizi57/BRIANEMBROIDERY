About Brian Embroidery

From a two-metre space in Kiyembe Lane to Jemba Plaza

How we started

Brian Embroidery started in 2013. My brother, Bukenya Brian, had worked for someone in Kitende for some years. He saved, bought a Brother embroidery machine, and gave it to his little brother Martin.

Martin had just finished Senior Four. He had come from Kayayumbe village in Kyotera District to Kampala. He had no shop. He started in Basajjabalaba Building on Kiyembe Lane, where they rented a space of about two metres, just enough for a desk and a chair, and started stitching.

That small start grew. Today we are at Jemba Plaza, Second Floor, Room K132, on Kiyembe Lane / Luwum Street in Kampala, with industrial embroidery machines. We still work the same way: sit with the customer, agree on the design and price, then stitch it properly.

What we do now

We stitch logos, names and designs on shirts, uniforms, caps and other clothing for individuals, schools, companies, churches and organisations. If you have artwork, we use it. If you only have an idea, we help you turn it into a design that can be stitched.

How we work

  1. You tell us what you need: the item, quantity, and design.
  2. We agree on price and days: days depend on workload, we tell you honestly before you pay.
  3. We stitch and check: every piece is checked before you collect it.
